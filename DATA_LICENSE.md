## Modifications and derived information

The source data were processed for research and planning purposes. Processing may include cleaning, filtering, coordinate transformation, spatial aggregation, grid assignment, density or risk estimation, travel-time and path calculations, optimization, and conversion to browser-ready formats. Files in the scenario, analysis, processed-data, and browser-layer directories may therefore differ from the original City datasets.

The source-data reference supplied with the project is retained under `data/Original Data/`. Public derived layers and model outputs are retained under `frontend/public/layers/` and should not be represented as official City of Toronto records. Intermediate analytical and model-development files are archived separately and are not required to operate the public webmap.
